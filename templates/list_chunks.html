About AI Embedding Chunks

AI embedding chunks are optimized text segments designed to be used with AI models for search, retrieval, and analysis.

Why use chunks?
  • Improves relevance in search and retrieval
  • Allows for more precise answers from AI models
  • Optimizes token usage with AI models
  • Preserves context while focusing on specific content
How to use these chunks
  • Each chunk has a unique ID and context information
  • Use chunks with vector databases (e.g., Pinecone, Weaviate)
  • Use with RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) systems
  • Download individual chunks or full sets for your AI workflows
