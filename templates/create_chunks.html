About AI Chunks

AI Embedding chunks are smaller pieces of your document optimized for use with AI models. These chunks:

  • Preserve the semantic meaning of your content
  • Include context headers to maintain document structure
  • Are sized appropriately for embedding models (typically 250-500 tokens)
  • Can have overlap to maintain context between chunks
tokens
Recommended: 250-500 tokens per chunk. Smaller chunks are more precise but may lose context. Larger chunks preserve more context but may be less relevant for specific queries.
%
Recommended: 10-20% overlap. This helps maintain continuity of context between chunks.

How Chunking Works
What determines good chunk size?
  • Short chunks (100-200 tokens): Best for simple definitions or facts without much context.
  • Medium chunks (250-500 tokens): Good balance of context and precision for most content.
  • Large chunks (500-1000 tokens): Best for complex topics that need more context to understand.
Context Preservation

Each chunk includes a context header with:

  • Document title and source
  • Section hierarchy (H1 > H2 > H3)
  • Overlap with adjacent chunks to maintain flow
