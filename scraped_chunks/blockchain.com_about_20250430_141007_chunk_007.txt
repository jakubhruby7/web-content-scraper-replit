“Manny” is the Founder of Kingsway Capital, an investment firm focusing on Frontier Emerging Markets. In Kingsway’s venture capital strategy, Kingsway is focusing on the disruptive impact of crypto or “digital assets”, which have become an important tool for financial inclusion globally, but of particular importance across the firm’s core markets. Manny has a degree in Economics from the London School of Economics (LSE) where he was awarded the “Stelios” Scholarship as well as a recipient of the German National Merit Award. He started his career at Goldman Sachs Investment Partners and was a Partner at London-based THS before starting Kingsway in 2013. Manny serves a on the Board of the Sohn Conference Foundation London and created the LSE “Pass The Torch” Scholarship programme for underprivileged students.
